Self-custody is not simply owning a hardware wallet. It is a system that joins
key control, backups, recovery instructions and an operating routine you can
follow under pressure.

## Start with the recovery path

Before choosing devices, decide who must be able to recover the bitcoin, under
which circumstances, and what knowledge they will need. A backup that has never
been tested is an assumption rather than a recovery plan.

## Match complexity to the risk

Every additional device, location and signer can reduce one risk while creating
another. Prefer the simplest arrangement that meets your actual threat model and
that you can rehearse safely.
